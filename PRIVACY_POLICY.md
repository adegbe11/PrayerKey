# PrayerKey Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

PrayerKey is designed for private prayer, Bible reading, journaling, fasting, and church notes.

## Information stored on the device

Prayer requests, journal entries, saved Scripture, challenge progress, reminder settings, and sermon notes are stored locally on the user's device. PrayerKey does not sell this information.

## Microphone

Microphone access is used only when the user starts sermon capture or another voice feature. PrayerKey does not activate the microphone without a user action. Android displays a foreground-service notification while long-form sermon capture is active.

## Sermon recordings and captions

Sermon capture writes an audio file and a transcript to PrayerKey's private
storage on the device. Neither is uploaded. Captions are produced by a speech
model that runs entirely on the device, so no audio is sent anywhere to be
transcribed, and capture works with no internet connection.

The speech model itself is downloaded once, on request, from the Vosk project's
public model host. That download carries no personal information — it is the
same file for every user, and the app asks before fetching it.

Recordings stay until deleted. Deleting a sermon note deletes its audio with it,
and uninstalling PrayerKey removes both.

## Notifications

Notifications are optional. Users can decline them during onboarding or disable them in PrayerKey or Android settings. Notification taps may open the relevant Bible, prayer, devotional, journal, fasting, or church screen.

## Network access

Some Bible, prayer, and church features may use PrayerKey services when an internet connection is available. Core Bible reading, bundled prayers, and local journal data remain available offline. Network providers may process technical connection information under their own policies.

## Data sharing and exports

PrayerKey shares content only when the user chooses an Android share or export action. Exported files leave PrayerKey's private storage and are controlled by the destination selected by the user.

## Deletion

Users can delete individual content in the app. Uninstalling PrayerKey removes locally stored app data, subject to the device's operating-system behavior.

## Children and sensitive use

PrayerKey is not an emergency, medical, legal, or financial service. Prayer content does not replace qualified professional care.
