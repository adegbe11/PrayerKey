PrayerKey is the prayer points app. About ten minutes with God, every day.

Some days you know exactly what to pray. Other days, you don't know where to begin.

PrayerKey gives you a place to start, and then gives you room to pray.

TODAY'S PRAYER POINTS

Every day begins with the Word, then praise, covering, your petitions, standing, and the seal.

Each point is read to you in a calm voice over quiet music. Then the screen goes still, so you can pray that point in your own words before the next one is read.

Stop halfway and PrayerKey remembers where you were.

THE PEOPLE YOU PROMISED TO PRAY FOR

Add the names of the people you are praying for. Today's prayer points pray for them by name, a few each day, until God answers. Mark it when He does.

SEASONS OF PRAYER

The January 21-day fast, Lent, Holy Week, the ten days to Pentecost, Advent, the crossover night, and a midnight prayer week every month. PrayerKey knows the season and opens what was made for it.

Everything essential works offline. There is no account to create and nothing to pay.

PRAY ABOUT WHAT YOU'RE FACING

Write what you want to pray about in your own words.

Your marriage, your children, your health, your work, your finances, a decision you have to make, or someone you love.

PrayerKey turns what you carry into a prayer you can actually pray.

If you'd rather choose, you can browse over 500 prayer situations and find the one that fits.

PRAY FOR FIVE MINUTES OR AN HOUR

Choose how long: 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes.

Use guided prayer with Scripture and prompts, lighter guidance when you need it, or a plain timer with no prompts at all.

PrayerKey gives you the structure. You do the praying.

MORNING AND EVENING PRAYER

Start the morning with Scripture and prayer. Come back in the evening to give thanks, put down what you're carrying, and pray before you sleep.

There is also midnight prayer, warfare prayer and prayer challenges when you want to go further.

A DAILY DEVOTIONAL, WRITTEN FOR EVERY DAY

366 days of devotionals, arranged as twelve monthly keys. Every day is written - not a template with the date changed.

Read the passage, sit with what it means, pray through it, and carry something from God's Word into your day.

THE WHOLE BIBLE, OFFLINE

The complete King James Version is inside the app. All 66 books, 31,102 verses, nothing to download and no connection needed.

Browse the Old and New Testaments, or listen to the audio Bible when you would rather hear the Word than read it.

FIND VERSES FOR WHAT YOU'RE FACING

Search by subject rather than reference: money and work, marriage, family, health, suffering, faith, prayer, spiritual warfare, character, church and leadership.

CHURCH NOTES

Take PrayerKey to church. It listens during the sermon and writes down what is said, so you can stay with the preaching instead of typing through it.

The recording never leaves your phone. Scripture references are caught as they are spoken, and the sermon becomes a note you can still read on Monday.

A JOURNAL

Write what you're praying for, what you're learning, or what you don't want to forget. Mark a prayer when it is answered. Look back in a year and see how much of it was.

FASTING AND REMINDERS

Set a fast with a rhythm you can keep, and get reminded at the hour you actually pray rather than the hour an app decided.

PRAYERKEY INCLUDES

- Today's prayer points, read to you, with time to pray
- Prayer for the people you promised to pray for
- Seasons of prayer, fasts and challenges
- Over 500 prayer situations
- Morning, evening and midnight prayer
- 366 written devotionals
- The whole King James Bible, offline, with audio
- Sermon notes transcribed on your phone
- A prayer journal for answered prayer

PrayerKey isn't here to pray instead of you. It reads you the point. You do the praying.

Pray. Read the Bible. Keep going.
