ash-wednesday|Return to Me|Joel 2:13|Lord,/rend my heart and not my garments./On this first day of Lent,/I turn back to You./You are gracious and merciful,/slow to anger and of great kindness./Walk with me these forty days./Amen.
palm-sunday|Hosanna|Matthew 21:9|Lord Jesus,/the crowds shouted, Hosanna, blessed is He that comes in the name of the Lord./I lay my palm branch before You today./Be King in my life,/not only on the easy days but all week long./Amen.
good-friday|It Is Finished|John 19:30|Jesus,/You said, It is finished,/and You bowed Your head and gave up Your spirit./Everything needed to save me was finished on that cross./I kneel in wonder today./Thank You for dying in my place./Amen.
easter|He Is Risen|Matthew 28:6|Risen Lord,/He is not here, for He is risen, as He said./The grave is empty and death has lost./Raise up in me every hope that died./Let me live today/in the power of Your resurrection./Hallelujah. Amen.
ascension|He Will Come Again|Acts 1:11|Lord Jesus,/You went up into heaven,/and You will come again in the same way./Help me live today ready for Your return,/working faithfully until I see You face to face./Amen.
pentecost|Come, Holy Spirit|Acts 2:4|Holy Spirit,/on the day of Pentecost You filled them all./Fill me again today./Set my heart on fire for God./Give me boldness to speak/and love to serve./Amen.
