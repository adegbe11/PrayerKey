12-01|Prepare the Way|Isaiah 40:3|Lord,/a voice cries, Prepare ye the way of the Lord./As Christmas comes near,/prepare my heart for You./Clear away the clutter and the rush,/and make room for joy./Amen.
12-02|Good Tidings|Isaiah 52:7|Lord,/how beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news./Make me a bringer of good news this month,/to my family, my neighbours and my friends./Let my words carry hope./Amen.
12-03|Joy Unspeakable|1 Peter 1:8|Lord Jesus,/I have not seen You,/yet I love You and rejoice with joy unspeakable./Fill me with that joy today,/the joy that does not depend on how my day goes./Amen.
12-04|The People Who Walked in Darkness|Isaiah 9:2|Lord,/the people who walked in darkness have seen a great light./Shine Your light on those sitting in darkness this season,/the lonely, the grieving and the lost./Amen.
12-05|A Child Is Born|Isaiah 9:6|Lord Jesus,/unto us a child is born,/Wonderful Counsellor, Mighty God,/Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace./Be all of these to me today./Amen.
12-06|Joy in Community|Acts 2:46-47|Father,/the early church ate together with gladness and singleness of heart./Bless my church family this season./Let us share meals, laughter and praise,/and let others want to join us./Amen.
12-07|Good Things to Come|Romans 15:13|God of hope,/fill me with all joy and peace in believing,/so that I may abound in hope/by the power of the Holy Spirit./Let my hope spill over today./Amen.
12-08|Emmanuel|Matthew 1:23|Lord,/they shall call His name Emmanuel, God with us./You are not far away./You came close./Let me feel Your nearness today/in every ordinary moment./Amen.
12-09|My Soul Magnifies|Luke 1:46-47|Lord,/like Mary, my soul magnifies You/and my spirit rejoices in God my Saviour./You have done great things for me./Let my life make You look bigger to others./Amen.
12-10|Joy of Salvation|Isaiah 12:3|Lord,/with joy shall I draw water out of the wells of salvation./Let me draw from those wells today./Refresh my joy in what Jesus has done for me./Amen.
12-11|Gentleness Known|Philippians 4:5|Lord,/let my gentleness be known to everyone./The Lord is at hand./In the rush of the season,/let me be gentle with the people around me./Amen.
12-12|Waiting with Hope|Luke 2:25|Lord,/Simeon waited for the consolation of Israel,/and the Holy Spirit was upon him./Teach me to wait for Your promises/with hope and expectation,/like he did./Amen.
12-13|Gifts of the Magi|Matthew 2:11|Lord Jesus,/the wise men opened their treasures and gave You gifts./What can I give You this Christmas?/I give You my heart,/my time and my worship./Amen.
12-14|Praise with Joy|Psalm 98:4|Lord,/I make a joyful noise to You./I break out in song and rejoice./Let my home be full of joyful praise this season,/not just noise and stress./Amen.
12-15|Good Will Toward Men|Luke 2:14|Lord,/glory to God in the highest,/and on earth peace, good will toward men./Let me spread peace and good will this season,/even to those who are hard to love./Amen.
12-16|For the Lonely at Christmas|Psalm 25:16|Lord,/turn to me and have mercy, for I am lonely and afflicted./I pray for everyone who feels alone this Christmas./Surround them with love/and let them know You are near./Amen.
12-17|The Word Became Flesh|John 1:14|Lord Jesus,/the Word was made flesh and dwelt among us./You moved into the neighbourhood./Thank You for coming close./Let me see Your glory, full of grace and truth./Amen.
12-18|Fullness of Joy|Psalm 16:11|Lord,/in Your presence is fullness of joy,/and at Your right hand are pleasures for evermore./Show me the path of life today/and let me walk it with joy./Amen.
12-19|Rejoicing Together|Romans 12:15|Lord,/help me rejoice with those who rejoice/and weep with those who weep./Make me present for my friends this season,/in their happiness and in their sorrow./Amen.
12-20|Joseph's Obedience|Matthew 1:24|Lord,/Joseph woke up and did what the angel commanded./He obeyed without fully understanding./Give me that quiet obedience,/even when Your plans surprise me./Amen.
12-21|The Shortest Days|John 1:5|Lord Jesus,/the light shines in the darkness,/and the darkness has not overcome it./On these short dark days,/let Your light burn bright in me./Amen.
12-22|Make Room|Luke 2:7|Lord Jesus,/there was no room for You in the inn./Do not let my life be so full that there is no room for You./Clear a space in my heart/and come in./Amen.
12-23|Shepherds Heard First|Luke 2:8-9|Lord,/You announced the birth of Jesus to ordinary shepherds working the night shift./Thank You that You come to ordinary people./Find me in my ordinary work today./Amen.
12-24|Silent Night|Luke 2:11|Lord,/unto us is born this day in the city of David a Saviour,/which is Christ the Lord./On this holy night,/quiet my heart to wonder again/at the gift of Your Son./Amen.
12-25|Christmas Day|Luke 2:10|Lord Jesus,/today the angel's words are true for me:/Fear not, I bring you good tidings of great joy./Happy birthday, King./Thank You for coming./Let joy fill my home today./Amen.
12-26|Treasure These Things|Luke 2:19|Lord,/Mary kept all these things and pondered them in her heart./After the busy day,/let me treasure what You showed me this Christmas/and keep it close./Amen.
12-27|They Returned Praising|Luke 2:20|Lord,/the shepherds returned glorifying and praising God/for all they had heard and seen./Let me go back to ordinary life/praising You for what I have seen./Amen.
12-28|Fellowship|1 John 1:3|Father,/our fellowship is with You and with Your Son Jesus./Thank You for the people I have shared this season with./Let our fellowship be real and our joy full./Amen.
12-29|Goodness and Mercy|Psalm 23:6|Lord,/surely goodness and mercy have followed me all the days of this year./Thank You./Let them follow me into the next one,/and let me dwell in Your house for ever./Amen.
12-30|Hitherto|1 Samuel 7:12|Lord,/Samuel set up a stone and called it Ebenezer,/saying, Hitherto hath the Lord helped us./I set up my stone today./You have helped me all the way this year./Amen.
12-31|Crossover|Isaiah 43:19|Faithful God,/thank You for carrying me through this year,/the good days and the hard ones./As I cross into a new year,/I bring You my hopes and my fears./Do a new thing, Lord./I am ready./Amen.
