03-01|Be Still|Psalm 46:10|Lord,/You say, Be still, and know that I am God./My mind is loud this morning./Quiet the noise inside me./Let me sit with You for one minute without asking for anything,/just knowing that You are God and I am not./Amen.
03-02|Cast Your Cares|1 Peter 5:7|Father,/I cast all my cares on You, because You care for me./Here is the worry about money,/the worry about my health,/the worry about the people I love./I put them in Your hands and I leave them there./Amen.
03-03|Peace That Guards|Philippians 4:6-7|Lord,/I will not be anxious about anything today./Instead I bring You everything in prayer, with thanks./Let Your peace, which passes all understanding,/stand guard over my heart and my mind/through Christ Jesus./Amen.
03-04|He Gives Sleep|Psalm 127:2|Father,/You give Your beloved sleep./Tonight, take the thoughts that keep me awake./I do not need to solve everything before I rest./Let me lie down in peace,/because You do not sleep and You are watching over me./Amen.
03-05|Come to Me|Matthew 11:28|Jesus,/You invite the weary and heavy laden to come to You./I am tired in my body and tired in my soul./I come now./Give me the rest You promised,/the kind that sleep alone cannot give./Amen.

03-08|Do Not Worry About Tomorrow|Matthew 6:34|Jesus,/You said tomorrow will worry about itself./I keep living in tomorrow's problems./Bring me back into today,/where Your grace is enough/and Your help is already here./Amen.
03-09|My Refuge|Psalm 91:1-2|Most High God,/I dwell in Your secret place/and rest in the shadow of the Almighty./You are my refuge and my fortress./In You I trust./Shelter me from every fear that rises today./Amen.

03-11|A Quiet Place|Mark 6:31|Jesus,/You told Your tired disciples to come apart and rest a while./Show me how to make a quiet place in my busy day./Help me turn off the noise/and find a few moments alone with You./Amen.
03-12|When My Heart Is Overwhelmed|Psalm 61:2|Lord,/from the end of the earth I cry to You/when my heart is overwhelmed./Lead me to the rock that is higher than I./Lift me above this flood of feelings/and set my feet on You./Amen.

03-15|Wait Patiently|Psalm 37:7|Lord,/teach me to rest in You and wait patiently./Do not let me fret over those who seem to prosper/while I wait./Your timing is not late./Let my soul be quiet while You work./Amen.
