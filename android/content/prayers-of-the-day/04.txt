04-01|All Things New|Revelation 21:5|Lord,/You sit on the throne and say, Behold, I make all things new./Not some things. All things./Take what is tired and worn in me/and begin again./Let this month be a season of renewal/in my soul, my home and my faith./Amen.
04-02|Renewed Day by Day|2 Corinthians 4:16|Father,/my body gets tired and my strength runs low,/but my inward man is renewed day by day./Renew me again this morning./Let what is happening inside me/be stronger than what is wearing me down outside./Amen.
04-03|Transformed Mind|Romans 12:2|Lord,/I do not want to be shaped by the world around me./Transform me by the renewing of my mind./Change the way I think about money, success and myself,/until I can see clearly/what Your good and perfect will is./Amen.
04-04|Spring Rain|Hosea 6:3|Lord,/I want to know You more and more./You come to me like the rain,/like the spring rain that waters the earth./Rain on the dry places in my heart today/and let something green begin to grow./Amen.
04-05|Restore the Joy|Psalm 51:12|Father,/restore to me the joy of Your salvation./I remember when my faith felt alive and fresh./Bring that joy back again./Hold me up with Your free spirit,/willing and glad to follow You./Amen.
04-06|The Seed Must Fall|John 12:24|Jesus,/a seed must fall into the ground and die/before it bears much fruit./Show me what needs to die in me,/the pride, the fear, the old way of doing things,/so that new life can grow./Amen.
04-07|Revive Me|Psalm 85:6|Lord,/will You not revive us again,/that Your people may rejoice in You?/Start the revival in me./Wake up what has gone to sleep in my faith./Let my church and my home rejoice in You again./Amen.
04-08|Put Off, Put On|Ephesians 4:22-24|Father,/help me take off the old self like a worn coat,/with its habits and lies and bitterness./Renew me in the spirit of my mind./Let me put on the new self You created,/true and holy and good./Amen.
04-09|The Potter's Hands|Jeremiah 18:4|Lord,/when the clay was spoiled in the potter's hand,/he did not throw it away. He made it again./I feel spoiled in places./Do not throw me away./Shape me again into something that pleases You./Amen.
04-10|Second Chances|Jonah 3:1|Lord,/the word came to Jonah a second time./Thank You for being the God of second chances./I ran from You before./Speak to me again,/and this time let me obey./Amen.
04-11|Growing in Grace|2 Peter 3:18|Lord Jesus,/I want to grow in grace and in the knowledge of You./Do not let me stay the same year after year./Grow my patience, my kindness and my understanding./Let this spring be a growing season in my soul./Amen.
04-12|Waiting for Fruit|James 5:7|Father,/the farmer waits patiently for the early and latter rain./Teach me to wait like that for growth in my life./I want change overnight,/but You grow things in seasons./Make me patient with my own growth./Amen.
04-13|Rivers in the Desert|Isaiah 35:1|Lord,/You make the wilderness glad and the desert bloom like a rose./Look at the dry places in my life./Make them blossom again./Let people see beauty where there was only dust,/and give You the glory./Amen.
04-14|The Gardener|John 15:2|Father,/You are the gardener of my life./When You prune me, it hurts,/but You only cut so I can bear more fruit./Help me trust Your hands,/even when You take away things I wanted to keep./Amen.
04-15|Seek and Find|Jeremiah 29:13|Lord,/You promise that I will find You/when I seek You with all my heart./I have been seeking You with half a heart./Today I give You the whole of it./Let me find You fresh again./Amen.
04-16|A Heart of Flesh|Ezekiel 36:26|Lord,/take away my heart of stone,/the part of me that has grown cold and hard./Give me a heart of flesh,/soft toward You and tender toward others./Put Your Spirit within me./Amen.
04-17|Living Water|John 4:14|Jesus,/You offer water that becomes a spring within me./I keep drinking from wells that leave me thirsty./Today I come to You./Let Your living water rise in me/and overflow into the lives around me./Amen.
04-18|Return to Me|Joel 2:12-13|Lord,/You say, Turn to me with all your heart./I have drifted, little by little./I turn back now./You are gracious and merciful, slow to anger and of great kindness./Receive me as I return./Amen.
04-19|From Glory to Glory|2 Corinthians 3:18|Lord,/as I look at You,/I am being changed into Your image from glory to glory./Let me spend time looking at You today,/in Your word and in prayer,/so that I slowly become more like You./Amen.
04-20|Thirsty Ground|Isaiah 44:3|Father,/You promise to pour water on the thirsty/and floods on the dry ground./I am thirsty for You./Pour out Your Spirit on me and on my children,/and let us spring up like grass by the streams./Amen.
04-21|Come Out of the Grave|John 11:43-44|Jesus,/You called Lazarus out of the grave,/and then told them to loose him and let him go./Call me out of whatever has buried me./Then loose me from the grave clothes,/the old habits that still bind me./Amen.
04-22|The Earth Is the Lord's|Psalm 24:1|Creator God,/the earth is Yours and everything in it./Thank You for spring, for flowers and new leaves./Teach me to care for Your creation/and to see Your hand in every growing thing./Amen.
04-23|Renewed Strength|Psalm 103:5|Lord,/You satisfy my mouth with good things,/so that my youth is renewed like the eagle's./When I feel old and tired in my faith,/renew me./Let me rise again with fresh strength./Amen.
04-24|Wash Me|Psalm 51:7|Lord,/wash me and I shall be whiter than snow./I bring You the stains I cannot remove myself./Make me clean again./Let me walk today/with the lightness of someone forgiven./Amen.
04-25|Grow Up into Him|Ephesians 4:15|Lord Jesus,/help me grow up into You in all things./Not only in what I know,/but in how I love and speak and live./Let me speak the truth in love/and grow a little more mature today./Amen.
04-26|Seasons|Ecclesiastes 3:1|Father,/to everything there is a season./You know which season I am in right now./Help me not to wish it away./Teach me what this season is meant to grow in me,/and let me trust You for the next one./Amen.
04-27|Deep Roots|Jeremiah 17:7-8|Lord,/blessed is the one who trusts in You./Make me like a tree whose roots go down to the river./When the heat comes, let me not be afraid./When the dry year comes,/let me keep bearing fruit./Amen.
04-28|A New Song|Psalm 40:3|Lord,/You have put a new song in my mouth./I am tired of singing the old songs of complaint./Teach me to praise You in fresh ways./Let many see it and fear,/and put their trust in You./Amen.
04-29|Fresh Every Morning|Zephaniah 3:17|Father,/You are in the midst of me, mighty to save./You rejoice over me with joy/and You rest in Your love./You even sing over me./Let me wake up tomorrow knowing that I am Your delight./Amen.
04-30|Spring Has Come|Song of Solomon 2:11-12|Lord,/the winter is past and the rain is over./The flowers appear on the earth./Thank You for the renewal You worked in me this month./Let what You planted keep growing,/and let me sing in this new season./Amen.
