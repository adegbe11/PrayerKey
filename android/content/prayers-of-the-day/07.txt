07-01|Teach Us to Pray|Luke 11:1|Lord Jesus,/the disciples asked You, Lord, teach us to pray./I ask You the same today./Teach me to pray not just with words,/but with honesty and faith./Make this a month where prayer becomes my home./Amen.
07-02|Into the Closet|Matthew 6:6|Father,/You see me in secret./Help me find a quiet place to shut the door and pray./I do not need to impress anyone./Meet me in the secret place,/where it is only You and me./Amen.
07-03|Pray Without Ceasing|1 Thessalonians 5:16-18|Lord,/teach me to rejoice always,/to pray without ceasing,/and to give thanks in everything./I cannot stop and kneel every hour,/but let prayer run under my whole day/like a quiet stream that never runs dry./Amen.
07-04|Hallowed Be Your Name|Matthew 6:9|Our Father in heaven,/hallowed be Your name./Before I ask You for anything,/I want to honour who You are./You are holy and good./Let my life make Your name great today./Amen.
07-05|Your Kingdom Come|Matthew 6:10|Father,/Your kingdom come, Your will be done/on earth as it is in heaven./Let Your will be done in my home today,/in my city and in my nation,/just as it is done in heaven./Amen.
07-06|Ask, Seek, Knock|Matthew 7:7|Lord,/You told me to ask, to seek and to knock./I have stopped asking for some things./Give me faith to ask again,/to keep seeking and to keep knocking./Amen.
07-07|The Spirit Helps|Romans 8:26|Holy Spirit,/sometimes I do not know what to pray./Thank You for helping me in my weakness/and praying for me with groanings too deep for words./Pray through me today./Amen.
07-08|Early in the Morning|Mark 1:35|Jesus,/You rose a great while before day and went to pray alone./If You needed time with the Father,/how much more do I./Wake me early,/hungry to meet with God./Amen.
07-09|Lift Up My Voice|Psalm 34:4|Lord,/I sought You, and You heard me,/and delivered me from all my fears./Thank You for every prayer You have answered./Let those memories build my faith/to pray boldly today./Amen.
07-10|Delight in the Word|Psalm 1:2|Lord,/I want to delight in Your word/and meditate on it day and night./Let Scripture be sweet to me,/not a duty I rush through./Speak to me as I read today./Amen.
07-11|The Persistent Widow|Luke 18:1|Jesus,/You told the parable of the widow who kept asking/so that we would always pray and not faint./I have been praying a long time for this./Keep me from giving up./Amen.
07-12|Abide in Prayer|John 15:7|Lord Jesus,/if I abide in You and Your words abide in me,/I can ask what I will./Let me stay close to You,/and let my prayers be shaped by Your words./Amen.
07-13|Worship in Spirit|John 4:24|Father,/You are Spirit, and You seek those who worship in spirit and in truth./Let my worship be real today,/not just songs but my whole heart turned to You./Amen.
07-14|Intercession|1 Timothy 2:1|Lord,/I bring before You my family, my friends and my leaders./I pray for those who are sick and those who are lost./Teach me to stand in the gap for others/and to love them in prayer./Amen.
07-15|Draw Me|Song of Solomon 1:4|Lord,/draw me, and I will run after You./I cannot even want You without Your help./Pull my heart toward You today./Make me hungry for Your presence./Amen.
07-16|Thirst for God|Psalm 42:1-2|Lord,/as the deer pants for the water brooks,/so my soul pants for You./I am thirsty for the living God./Let nothing else satisfy me/until I have been with You./Amen.
07-17|Confess and Be Healed|James 5:16|Lord,/You tell us to confess our faults to one another/and pray for one another, so that we may be healed./Give me the humility to be honest,/and a friend I can pray with./Amen.
07-18|The Prayer of Faith|Mark 11:24|Lord Jesus,/You said whatever I ask in prayer, believe that I receive it./Increase my faith as I pray./Let me pray expecting You to answer,/not just hoping You might./Amen.
07-19|My Prayer Like Incense|Psalm 141:2|Lord,/let my prayer come before You like incense,/and the lifting of my hands like the evening sacrifice./Receive my prayer tonight./Let it rise to You like a sweet fragrance./Amen.
07-20|Gethsemane|Luke 22:42|Father,/Jesus prayed, Not my will, but thine, be done./I bring You what I want today./But more than that, I want Your will./Give me the strength to pray as He did./Amen.
07-21|Listen|Psalm 85:8|Lord,/I will hear what You will speak./I talk so much in prayer./Teach me to listen./Speak peace to Your people,/and let me be still enough to hear it./Amen.
07-22|Two or Three|Matthew 18:20|Jesus,/where two or three gather in Your name, You are there./Thank You for praying friends./Bless the people I pray with./Let us agree in prayer and see You move./Amen.
07-23|Pour Out Your Heart|Psalm 62:8|Lord,/You tell me to pour out my heart before You./I do not have to tidy up my feelings first./Here is everything I feel today./You are a refuge for me./Amen.
07-24|Hannah's Prayer|1 Samuel 1:15|Lord,/Hannah poured out her soul before You in tears./You heard her and answered./I bring my deepest longing to You today./Hear me as You heard her./Amen.
07-25|Watch and Pray|Matthew 26:41|Lord,/the spirit is willing but the flesh is weak./Help me watch and pray so I do not fall into temptation./When I am tired, keep my heart awake to You./Amen.
07-26|Praise Before the Answer|Acts 16:25|Lord,/Paul and Silas prayed and sang praises at midnight in prison./Teach me to praise You in my midnight./Before the chains fall and the doors open,/let my heart sing to You/in the dark./Amen.
07-27|Your Face I Seek|Psalm 27:8|Lord,/when You said, Seek my face,/my heart said to You, Your face, Lord, will I seek./I do not only want Your answers./I seek more than Your gifts./I seek You,/and You are enough./Amen.
07-28|Every Thought Captive|2 Corinthians 10:5|Lord,/bring every thought into captivity/to the obedience of Christ./When my mind wanders during prayer,/gently bring it back to You./Silence the anxious thoughts and the proud ones,/and fill my mind with Your truth./Amen.
07-29|The Lord's Presence|Exodus 33:14|Lord,/You said, My presence shall go with thee, and I will give thee rest./I do not want to go anywhere without You./Let Your presence go with me today./Amen.
07-30|Draw Near with Confidence|Hebrews 4:16|Father,/I come boldly to Your throne of grace,/not because I am good,/but because Jesus made the way./Let me find mercy and grace to help in my time of need./Amen.
07-31|A House of Prayer|Isaiah 56:7|Lord,/Your house shall be called a house of prayer for all people./Make my home a house of prayer./Make my heart a house of prayer./Thank You for drawing me deeper this month./Amen.
