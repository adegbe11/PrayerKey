08-01|The Beginning of Wisdom|Proverbs 9:10|Lord,/the fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom./Before I am clever, let me be reverent./Teach me to honour You first in every choice this month,/and let wisdom grow from there./Amen.
08-02|Wisdom from Above|James 3:17|Father,/the wisdom from above is pure, peaceable and gentle,/easy to be entreated and full of mercy./Give me that kind of wisdom today,/not the kind that wins arguments,/but the kind that brings peace./Amen.
08-03|Solomon's Request|1 Kings 3:9|Lord,/Solomon asked for an understanding heart/to know good from bad./I ask the same today./Give me a listening heart/for the people and the choices in front of me./Amen.
08-04|Counsel|Proverbs 11:14|Father,/where there is no counsel the people fall,/but in many counsellors there is safety./Bring wise people into my life./Give me the humility to ask for advice/and the sense to take it./Amen.
08-05|The Narrow Way|Matthew 7:13-14|Lord Jesus,/the gate is narrow and the way is hard that leads to life./Many choose the easy road./Give me courage to choose Your way today,/even when it is the harder one./Amen.
08-06|Guard My Heart|Proverbs 4:23|Lord,/help me keep my heart with all diligence,/for out of it flow the issues of life./Guard what I watch, what I listen to/and who I allow to shape me./Amen.
08-07|Slow to Speak|James 1:19|Lord,/make me swift to hear, slow to speak and slow to wrath./I often speak before I think./Today, help me listen first,/pause before I answer,/and let my words be wise./Amen.
08-08|Choose This Day|Joshua 24:15|Lord,/Joshua said, Choose you this day whom ye will serve./As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord./I make that choice again today/for myself and for my home./Amen.
08-09|Money Wisdom|Proverbs 3:9-10|Lord,/everything I have comes from You./Teach me to honour You with my money/and with the first of all my increase./Make me wise with spending and generous in giving./Free me from the love of money./Amen.
08-10|Which Way to Go|Isaiah 30:21|Lord,/You promise that my ears will hear a word behind me saying,/This is the way, walk ye in it./I am at a crossroads./Speak clearly,/and give me courage to turn when You say turn./Amen.
08-11|Count the Cost|Luke 14:28|Lord Jesus,/You told us to sit down and count the cost before we build./Keep me from rushing into big decisions./Give me patience to plan/and wisdom to finish what I start./Amen.
08-12|Wise Friends|Proverbs 13:20|Father,/he that walks with wise men shall be wise./Show me who I am walking with./Give me friends who draw me closer to You,/and make me a wise friend to others./Amen.
08-13|Discernment|Philippians 1:9-10|Lord,/let my love grow in knowledge and in all judgment,/so I can tell what is excellent./Give me discernment about what I read and hear,/and keep me sincere and without offence./Amen.
08-14|Built on the Rock|Matthew 7:24|Jesus,/the wise man hears Your words and does them./He builds his house on the rock./Let me not only hear Your word today but do it,/so that when storms come, my house stands./Amen.
08-15|A Wise Tongue|Proverbs 18:21|Lord,/death and life are in the power of the tongue./Let me speak life today./Over my children, my spouse, my friends and myself,/let my words build up and bless./Amen.
08-16|Walk Circumspectly|Ephesians 5:15-16|Lord,/help me walk carefully, not as a fool but as wise,/redeeming the time./Do not let me waste my hours on what does not matter./Show me how to use today well./Amen.
08-17|Mind of Christ|1 Corinthians 2:16|Lord Jesus,/I have the mind of Christ./When I face a decision today,/let me think the way You think./Let Your thoughts guide my choices/instead of fear or pride./Amen.
08-18|Patience in Decisions|Proverbs 19:2|Father,/he that hastens with his feet sins./Keep me from hasty choices made in panic./Slow me down until I have prayed./Let me act from peace and not from pressure./Amen.
08-19|Simple Obedience|1 Samuel 15:22|Lord,/to obey is better than sacrifice./I do not need to do something grand for You today./Help me simply obey what You have already told me,/in the small things right in front of me./Amen.
08-20|Hidden Treasure|Proverbs 2:3-5|Lord,/I cry out for understanding/and search for it like hidden treasure./Let me dig into Your word today/the way people dig for silver,/and let me find You there./Amen.
08-21|The Fool Says|Psalm 14:1|Lord,/the fool says in his heart, There is no God./Keep me from living as if You are not watching./Let my private choices show that I believe You are real/and that You see./Amen.
08-22|Be Not Wise in Your Own Eyes|Proverbs 3:7|Lord,/keep me from being wise in my own eyes./When I am sure I am right, humble me./Teach me to fear You and to depart from evil,/and let me stay teachable all my life./Amen.
08-23|Light for the Path|Psalm 119:130|Lord,/the entrance of Your words gives light./It gives understanding to the simple./I feel simple and unsure today./Open Your word to me and let it light my way./Amen.
08-24|Generous Wisdom|Proverbs 11:25|Father,/the liberal soul shall be made fat,/and he that waters shall be watered himself./Make me wise enough to be generous./Let me pour into others/and trust You to refill me./Amen.
08-25|Integrity|Proverbs 10:9|Lord,/he that walks uprightly walks surely./Give me integrity when no one is watching./Let my yes be yes and my no be no./Keep me honest in small things and in big things./Amen.
08-26|Wise as Serpents|Matthew 10:16|Jesus,/You sent us out as sheep among wolves./Make me wise as a serpent and harmless as a dove./Give me street sense and a pure heart,/so I am neither naive nor cruel./Amen.
08-27|Teachable Spirit|Proverbs 12:1|Lord,/whoever loves instruction loves knowledge./Give me a teachable spirit./When I am corrected, let me listen instead of defending myself,/and grow because of it./Amen.
08-28|The Wise Woman Builds|Proverbs 14:1|Lord,/every wise woman builds her house,/but the foolish plucks it down with her hands./Help me build my home with wisdom,/with patience and kind words,/one day at a time./Amen.
08-29|Let Peace Decide|Colossians 3:15|Lord,/let the peace of God rule in my heart./When I cannot decide, let Your peace be my guide./Where there is no peace, let me wait./And give me a thankful heart while I do./Amen.
08-30|A Wise Heart Rejoices|Proverbs 23:15|Father,/thank You for the wisdom You have given me this month./Keep growing it in me./Let my choices bring You joy,/and let my heart be glad that I chose Your way./Amen.
08-31|Higher Wisdom|Romans 11:33|Lord,/O the depth of the riches both of Your wisdom and knowledge!/How unsearchable are Your judgments./I end this month in wonder./You are wiser than all my best plans./Amen.
