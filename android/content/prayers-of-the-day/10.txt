10-01|Pray for My Nation|Jeremiah 29:7|Lord,/You told Your people to seek the peace of the city where they lived/and to pray for it./Today I pray for my nation./Give our leaders wisdom and honesty./Let there be peace, justice and bread for all./Amen.
10-02|Count It All Joy|James 1:2-4|Lord,/You tell me to count it all joy when I face trials./That is hard to do./Let patience have its perfect work in me,/so that I come out of this trial complete,/lacking nothing./Amen.
10-03|Endure Hardness|2 Timothy 2:3|Lord Jesus,/help me endure hardness as a good soldier./Not every day is easy./Give me the discipline to keep going/when I want to give up./Amen.
10-04|Not Forsaken|2 Corinthians 4:9|Lord,/I am persecuted but not forsaken,/struck down but not destroyed./Life has knocked me down./But I am still here, and You are still with me./Help me get up again./Amen.
10-05|Job's Faith|Job 13:15|Lord,/Job said, Though he slay me, yet will I trust in him./I do not understand what is happening./But I choose to trust You still,/even here./Amen.
10-06|The Lord Is Near|Psalm 34:18|Lord,/You are near to the brokenhearted/and You save those of a crushed spirit./I am crushed today./Come near./Hold me close./Amen.
10-07|Weeping Endures a Night|Psalm 30:5|Father,/weeping may endure for a night,/but joy comes in the morning./I am still in the night./Give me strength to wait for the morning/You have promised./Amen.
10-08|Patient in Tribulation|Romans 12:12|Lord,/teach me to rejoice in hope,/to be patient in tribulation,/and to keep praying./When the trouble goes on and on,/let my prayers go on longer./Amen.
10-09|Light Affliction|2 Corinthians 4:17|Lord,/this affliction feels heavy,/but You call it light and short/compared with the glory that is coming./Help me see my trouble/from the view of eternity./Amen.
10-10|Refined Like Gold|1 Peter 1:7|Father,/the trial of my faith is more precious than gold/tried in the fire./Use this fire to purify me,/not destroy me./Let my faith come out stronger./Amen.
10-11|Keep Going|Hebrews 10:36|Lord,/I need patience so that after I have done Your will,/I may receive the promise./I am close to quitting./Give me patience for one more day/and then the next./Amen.
10-12|Joseph's Waiting|Psalm 105:19|Lord,/Joseph waited until the time that his word came./Your word tested him while he waited./Keep me faithful in my waiting room/until Your time comes./Amen.
10-13|In the Lions' Den|Daniel 6:22|Lord,/You sent Your angel and shut the lions' mouths./Shut the mouths of the lions around me today:/the threats, the lies, the fears./Keep me safe in the den/until You bring me out./Amen.
10-14|Do Not Fret|Psalm 37:1|Lord,/keep me from fretting because of evildoers/or being envious of those who seem to win by doing wrong./You see all of it./I leave justice in Your hands./Amen.
10-15|He Holds My Tears|Psalm 56:8|Lord,/You keep count of my wanderings/and put my tears in Your bottle./Not one tear has been wasted./You saw every one./Comfort me today./Amen.
10-16|Hope That Anchors|Hebrews 6:19|Lord Jesus,/my hope in You is an anchor for my soul,/sure and steadfast./When the storm pulls at me,/let this anchor hold./Amen.
10-17|Not in Vain|1 Corinthians 15:58|Father,/make me steadfast and unmovable,/always abounding in Your work./Some days it feels like nothing I do for You matters./Remind me that my labour is not in vain in the Lord./Amen.
10-18|Wilderness Bread|Deuteronomy 8:3|Lord,/You fed Your people with manna in the wilderness/so they would learn that man does not live by bread alone./Feed me today with Your word./Teach me in this dry place./Amen.
10-19|The Lord Will Perfect|Psalm 138:8|Lord,/You will perfect what concerns me./Your mercy endures for ever./Do not abandon the work of Your hands./Finish what You began in my life./Amen.
10-20|Strength Renewed|Isaiah 40:28|Lord,/You do not faint and You are never weary./There is no limit to Your understanding./When my strength runs out,/Yours does not./Lend me Yours today./Amen.
10-21|Paul's Contentment|Philippians 4:11-12|Lord,/Paul learned to be content in every state,/in plenty and in need./Teach me that secret./Whether I have much or little today,/let me be content in You./Amen.
10-22|After You Have Suffered|1 Peter 5:10|Father,/the God of all grace,/after I have suffered a while,/make me perfect, establish, strengthen and settle me./Let this season end in strength./Amen.
10-23|Wait for It|Habakkuk 2:3|Lord,/the vision is for an appointed time./Though it tarries, I will wait for it./It will surely come./Keep my hope alive while I wait./Amen.
10-24|Through Many Tribulations|Acts 14:22|Lord,/the apostles taught that we must enter the kingdom through much tribulation./I am not surprised by trouble anymore./But I ask for strength to continue in the faith./Amen.
10-25|Unseen Things|2 Corinthians 4:18|Lord,/help me look not at the things which are seen,/but at the things which are not seen./What I see is temporary./What You are doing is eternal./Amen.
10-26|Ruth's Loyalty|Ruth 1:16|Lord,/Ruth stayed when it would have been easier to leave./Give me that kind of loyalty/to You and to the people I love,/even in hard seasons./Amen.
10-27|Still Faithful|2 Timothy 2:13|Lord,/even when I am faithless, You remain faithful./You cannot deny Yourself./When my faith is weak today,/lean on Your faithfulness./Amen.
10-28|Night Songs|Job 35:10|Lord,/You give songs in the night./It is dark right now./Give me a song anyway,/a small song of trust/to carry me until morning./Amen.
10-29|The Crown|James 1:12|Lord,/blessed is the one who endures temptation./When he is tried, he shall receive the crown of life./Help me endure today,/and keep my eyes on the crown./Amen.
10-30|He Knows the Way|Job 23:10|Lord,/You know the way that I take./When You have tried me, I shall come forth as gold./I may not know the way,/but You do./Amen.
10-31|Finish the Race|2 Timothy 4:7|Lord,/Paul could say, I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course./Thank You for carrying me through this month./Help me keep the faith to the end./Amen.
