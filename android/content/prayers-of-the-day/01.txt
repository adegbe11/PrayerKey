01-01|A New Year with God|Lamentations 3:22-23|Faithful God,/before this year has made a single promise,/You have already kept Yours./Your mercies are new this morning,/and they will be new every morning after it./I give You the days I can see/and the ones I cannot./Walk ahead of me into this year,/and let me end it closer to You than I began./Amen.
01-02|Order My Steps|Psalm 37:23|Lord,/the steps of a good man are ordered by You,/so I bring You my feet before I bring You my plans./Where I would rush, slow me down./Where I would stop, give me courage to keep walking./Let every step today be one You have ordered,/and let me delight in the way You lead./Amen.
01-03|First Things First|Matthew 6:33|Father,/I want to seek You first,/not after the phone, the news and the worries./Be the first voice I hear today./Put Your kingdom at the top of my list/and let everything else find its right place under it./I trust You with all these things I need./Amen.
01-04|A Clean Heart|Psalm 51:10|Create in me a clean heart, O God./Wash away what I carried into this new season/that does not belong to You./Renew a right spirit within me,/steady and willing and true./Let my heart be a place You are glad to live in./In Jesus' name. Amen.
01-05|Morning Prayer|Psalm 5:3|Lord,/in the morning You hear my voice./Before the noise of the day begins,/I lay my prayer before You/and I look up, expecting You to answer./Teach me the habit of meeting You first,/so that wherever this day takes me,/I am already Yours./Amen.
01-06|The Light Has Come|Isaiah 60:1|Lord Jesus,/the wise men followed a star until they found You,/and the light of the world was a child in a house./Arise in me today and let me shine./Let Your glory rise upon my home, my work and my words,/so that someone looking for You/finds their way because of me./Amen.
01-07|Little by Little|Zechariah 4:10|Father,/do not let me despise the day of small things./The small prayer, the short reading, the quiet minute:/You see them and You are pleased./Build my life with You one faithful day at a time,/until what was small becomes strong./Amen.
01-08|Write the Vision|Habakkuk 2:2|Lord,/give me a vision for this year that comes from You./Make it plain enough to write down/and clear enough to run with./Where my plans are only mine, change them./Where they are Yours, give me patience to wait for them,/for they will surely come./Amen.
01-09|Teach Me to Number My Days|Psalm 90:12|Eternal God,/teach me to number my days,/so that I do not waste the ones You give me./Let me not spend this year on what does not last./Give me a heart of wisdom/that knows what matters to You/and gives its best hours to it./Amen.
01-10|A Lamp for My Feet|Psalm 119:105|Lord,/Your word is a lamp to my feet./I do not need to see the whole road,/only the next step, lit by You./Give me a hunger for Scripture this year./Let me open it daily,/and let it light the way I walk./Amen.
01-11|Forget the Former Things|Isaiah 43:18-19|Father,/You tell me not to keep staring at the former things./You are doing a new thing,/and it is already springing up./Open my eyes to see it./Make a way where I see no way,/and rivers where my life feels dry./Amen.
01-12|In Quietness and Confidence|Isaiah 30:15|Holy God,/my strength is not in hurry but in quietness and confidence in You./Teach me to return and rest./When I want to fix everything at once,/let me first sit with You./Grow in me a calm that comes from trusting You./Amen.
01-13|Commit Your Way|Psalm 37:5|Lord,/I commit my way to You today:/my work, my family, my money, my fears./I trust You with what I cannot control./Bring to pass what is good,/and close the doors that would harm me./My way is safer in Your hands than in mine./Amen.
01-14|A Willing Heart|Isaiah 6:8|Lord,/when You ask, Whom shall I send,/let me be quick to answer, Here am I./I do not need to be great to be useful to You,/only willing./Send me where I am needed today,/even if it is only across the room./Amen.
01-15|Abide in the Vine|John 15:5|Jesus,/You are the vine and I am a branch./Without You I can do nothing that lasts./Keep me close to You today,/drawing life from You and not from my own strength./Let my life bear fruit that others can taste./Amen.
01-16|One Thing I Ask|Psalm 27:4|Lord,/one thing I ask above all others:/to dwell in Your house all the days of my life,/to behold Your beauty and to seek You./When I want many things, bring me back to this one./Let being near You be enough for me./Amen.
01-17|Fresh Discipline|1 Timothy 4:7-8|Father,/I train my body for this life;/help me also train my soul for godliness./Build habits in me that make room for You:/prayer, Scripture, rest and kindness./Make these practices a joy and not a chore,/for they hold promise for this life and the next./Amen.
01-18|Wait on the Lord|Isaiah 40:31|Lord,/I am waiting on You for things I cannot rush./Renew my strength while I wait./Let me rise up like an eagle above discouragement,/run and not be weary,/walk and not faint./You are worth waiting for./Amen.
01-19|Trust Without Understanding|Proverbs 3:5-6|Lord,/I trust You with all my heart,/even the parts that do not understand./I will not lean on my own understanding today./In every decision I will acknowledge You,/and I believe You will make my path straight./Amen.
01-20|Your Word in My Heart|Psalm 119:11|Lord,/hide Your word in my heart/so that it is there when I need it most./Let a verse I read today come back to me/in the moment of temptation or fear./Fill my memory with Your truth/until it shapes how I think./Amen.
01-21|Strength for the Journey|Deuteronomy 31:8|Lord,/You go before me and You are with me./You will not fail me or forsake me./So I will not fear and I will not be dismayed./Whatever is ahead in this year,/You are already there./Amen.
01-22|Search Me|Psalm 139:23-24|Search me, O God, and know my heart./Show me anything in me that grieves You,/the attitude I defend and the habit I excuse./Do not leave me as I am./Lead me in the way everlasting,/gently and surely./Amen.
01-23|Daily Bread|Matthew 6:11|Father,/give me this day my daily bread./Not a year's supply, just enough for today,/so that I come back to You tomorrow./Provide for my needs and for those I love./Teach me to trust You one day at a time./Amen.
01-24|Do Not Grow Weary|Galatians 6:9|Lord,/I am tempted to give up on the good I started./Keep me from growing weary in doing well./Remind me that harvest comes in due season/if I do not lose heart./Give me strength to keep sowing today./Amen.
01-25|A New Creation|2 Corinthians 5:17|Jesus,/in You I am a new creation./The old has gone and the new has come./Let me stop living as if I am still the old me./Help me walk today in who You have made me,/forgiven, free and made new./Amen.
01-26|Planted by the Water|Psalm 1:3|Lord,/make me like a tree planted by rivers of water,/rooted deep, bearing fruit in its season,/with leaves that do not wither./When dry days come, keep me drinking from You./Let whatever I do prosper in Your hands./Amen.
01-27|Faithful in Little|Luke 16:10|Father,/teach me to be faithful in the little things:/the promise I made, the task no one sees,/the money that is not much./Let my faithfulness in small things/prepare me for whatever You trust me with next./Amen.
01-28|Draw Near|James 4:8|Lord,/You promise that if I draw near to You,/You will draw near to me./So here I am, coming closer./Meet me in this moment./Let me feel Your nearness today/in the ordinary places of my life./Amen.
01-29|Run the Race|Hebrews 12:1-2|Jesus,/help me lay aside every weight that slows me down/and the sin that so easily trips me./Let me run with patience the race set before me,/looking to You,/the author and finisher of my faith./Amen.
01-30|Whatever You Do|Colossians 3:23|Lord,/whatever I do today, let me do it heartily, as for You./The emails, the cooking, the cleaning, the meetings:/let all of it be worship./When no one notices my work,/remind me that You do./Amen.
01-31|He Who Began a Good Work|Philippians 1:6|Faithful Father,/as this first month ends,/I thank You for every step You helped me take./You began a good work in me/and You will carry it on to completion./I am not finished, but I am in Your hands./Amen.
