02-01|Loved Before I Loved|1 John 4:19|Father,/I love You because You first loved me./Before I knew Your name, You knew mine./Let this month teach me how loved I am,/so that I can love others from a full heart/and not an empty one./Amen.
02-02|Fearfully Made|Psalm 139:14|Creator God,/I am fearfully and wonderfully made./When I look in the mirror and see only flaws,/remind me that You made me on purpose./Heal the way I speak about myself./Let me praise You for the work of Your hands, even me./Amen.
02-03|Love Is Patient|1 Corinthians 13:4|Lord,/love is patient and love is kind,/and I am not always either./Give me patience with the people closest to me/and kindness when I am tired./Let my love look more like Yours today,/slow to anger and quick to be gentle./Amen.
02-04|Nothing Can Separate Us|Romans 8:38-39|Lord Jesus,/nothing can separate me from Your love:/not death, not life, not my past,/not what I fear about tomorrow./When I feel far from You,/hold me by this promise./I am Yours and I am loved./Amen.
02-05|Healing for Broken Hearts|Psalm 147:3|Healer,/You heal the brokenhearted and bind up their wounds./You know the places in me that still hurt,/the words that cut and the people who left./Bind up what is broken,/gently and fully, in Your time./Amen.
02-06|A Gentle Answer|Proverbs 15:1|Lord,/a soft answer turns away wrath./Before I reply in anger today, stop my tongue./Give me gentle words in hard conversations./Let my home be a place where voices are calm/and hearts feel safe./Amen.
02-07|Bear One Another|Galatians 6:2|Father,/show me whose burden I can help carry today./Make me alert to the friend who is quietly struggling./Let me give my time, my ear and my prayers,/and so fulfil the law of Christ./Amen.
02-08|For My Marriage|Ecclesiastes 4:12|Lord,/a cord of three strands is not quickly broken./Be the third strand in my marriage,/or in the one I am praying for./Bind us together in You./Where we have pulled apart, draw us close again./Amen.
02-09|Forgive as Forgiven|Colossians 3:13|Jesus,/You forgave me completely,/so teach me to forgive the one who hurt me./I cannot do it in my own strength./Soften what has become hard in me./Let me release the debt as You released mine./Amen.
02-10|For My Children|Isaiah 54:13|Father,/I bring my children before You,/the ones I have and the ones I hope for./Teach them Yourself./Let great be their peace./Guard their hearts, their friendships and their futures./Amen.
02-11|Love Your Neighbour|Mark 12:31|Lord,/You told me to love my neighbour as myself./Show me my neighbour today:/the one next door, at work, in the market,/the stranger who needs a kind word./Let my love be practical and real./Amen.
02-12|Clothed in Love|Colossians 3:14|Father,/above all things I want to put on love,/the bond that holds everything together./When I dress this morning,/let me put on kindness and humility too./Let people feel Your love when they are near me./Amen.
02-13|Honour One Another|Romans 12:10|Lord,/give me a heart that honours others above myself./Let me celebrate the success of my friends/without envy or comparison./Teach me to be warm and devoted/to the people You have placed around me./Amen.
02-14|God Is Love|1 John 4:16|God of love,/today the world talks about love,/but You are love itself./Thank You for every person who has loved me well./Bless those who feel alone today./Let them know they are seen and dearly loved by You./Amen.
02-15|Rooted in Love|Ephesians 3:17-19|Lord Jesus,/dwell in my heart by faith./Root me and ground me in Your love,/so I can grasp how wide and long and high and deep it is./Fill me with the fullness of God,/more than I can understand./Amen.
02-16|For My Friends|Proverbs 17:17|Father,/thank You for friends who love at all times./Bless the friends who have stood by me in hard seasons./Make me that kind of friend,/faithful when it is costly,/and present when it is inconvenient./Amen.
02-17|When I Feel Unlovable|Jeremiah 31:3|Lord,/You have loved me with an everlasting love./Not a love that runs out when I fail,/but one that draws me back with kindness./When I feel unlovable,/let Your everlasting love speak louder./Amen.
02-18|Peacemaker|Matthew 5:9|Prince of Peace,/blessed are the peacemakers./Use me to bring peace where there is tension:/in my family, my church, my workplace./Give me wisdom to know when to speak/and when to simply listen./Amen.
02-19|Speak Life|Ephesians 4:29|Lord,/let no corrupt word come out of my mouth today./Let my words build up and not tear down./Give me something good to say/to the person who needs it most,/words that carry grace./Amen.
02-20|The Prodigal Returns|Luke 15:20|Father,/You ran to the son who came home./I pray for the one I love who has wandered far./Bring them to their senses and turn them toward home./And when they come,/give me a heart that runs to meet them too./Amen.
02-21|Love in Action|1 John 3:18|Lord,/let me not love only in word,/but in deed and in truth./Show me one practical way to love someone today:/a meal, a visit, a call, a gift./Let my love be something people can touch./Amen.
02-22|Accepted in the Beloved|Ephesians 1:6|Father,/I am accepted in the Beloved./I do not have to earn Your approval./I am not on probation with You./Let this truth settle the anxious part of me/that is always trying to prove itself./Amen.
02-23|Healing Family Wounds|Malachi 4:6|Lord,/turn the hearts of fathers to their children/and children to their fathers./Heal the old wounds in my family line./Where there is silence, bring conversation./Where there is coldness, bring warmth again./Amen.
02-24|Kind to the Unkind|Luke 6:35|Lord Jesus,/You are kind to the unthankful and the evil,/so help me be kind to the person who is unkind to me./Guard my heart from bitterness./Let my response surprise them/and point them to You./Amen.
02-25|Love Covers|1 Peter 4:8|Father,/fervent love covers a multitude of sins./Help me cover the faults of others/instead of exposing them./Let me not keep a record of wrongs./Make my love deep enough to forgive often./Amen.
02-26|For Those Who Are Single|Psalm 68:6|Lord,/You set the lonely in families./I pray for every single heart that longs for companionship./Fill them with Your presence now,/surround them with true friends,/and lead them in Your perfect time./Amen.
02-27|Lay Down My Life|John 15:13|Jesus,/greater love has no one than this,/that a man lay down his life for his friends./You did that for me./Teach me to lay down my pride, my time, my comfort,/for the people You have given me to love./Amen.
02-28|Love Never Fails|1 Corinthians 13:8|Lord,/everything else will pass away,/but love never fails./Let what I build this year be built in love./Thank You for teaching me this month/that Your love is the key to every relationship./Amen.
02-29|A Day of Grace|Psalm 118:24|Father,/this extra day is a gift from You,/a day that only comes once in four years./Let me not waste it./I will rejoice and be glad in it,/and give it back to You with thanks./Amen.
