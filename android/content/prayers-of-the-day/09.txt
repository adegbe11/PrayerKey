09-01|Saved by Grace|Ephesians 2:8-9|Father,/by grace I am saved through faith,/and it is not from me. It is Your gift./I did not earn it and I cannot lose it by trying too hard./Let this month teach me to live in grace./Amen.
09-02|Grace Upon Grace|John 1:16|Lord Jesus,/out of Your fullness I have received grace upon grace./Wave after wave of it./When I feel I have used up Your patience,/remind me there is always more grace./Amen.
09-03|Forgiving Myself|Psalm 103:12|Father,/as far as the east is from the west,/so far have You removed my sins from me./If You have let them go,/help me stop digging them up./Teach me to forgive myself./Amen.
09-04|Seventy Times Seven|Matthew 18:21-22|Jesus,/Peter asked how many times he should forgive./You said, seventy times seven./I am tired of forgiving this person./Give me grace to forgive again,/and again, as You forgive me./Amen.
09-05|Where Sin Abounded|Romans 5:20|Lord,/where sin abounded, grace did much more abound./My failures are real,/but Your grace is bigger./Let me stand today in grace/and not in shame./Amen.
09-06|Grace for the Humble|James 4:6|Father,/You resist the proud but give grace to the humble./Humble me today,/not with shame but with honesty./Let me receive the grace You give/to those who know they need it./Amen.
09-07|Throw the First Stone|John 8:7|Jesus,/You told the crowd, He that is without sin, let him first cast a stone./Keep me from judging others harshly./Let me remember my own need of grace/before I point at anyone else./Amen.
09-08|Go and Sin No More|John 8:11|Jesus,/You said to the woman, Neither do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more./Thank You for not condemning me./Give me the strength to walk away from the sin/You have already forgiven./Amen.
09-09|Mercy Not Sacrifice|Matthew 9:13|Lord,/You desire mercy and not sacrifice./I try to impress You with effort,/but You want a merciful heart./Make me merciful today,/to others and to myself./Amen.
09-10|Clean Slate|1 John 1:9|Father,/if I confess my sins, You are faithful and just to forgive me/and to cleanse me from all unrighteousness./I confess them now./Thank You for a clean slate this morning./Amen.
09-11|Let Go of Bitterness|Ephesians 4:31-32|Lord,/I put away all bitterness, wrath and anger./I do not want to carry them anymore./Make me kind and tenderhearted./Help me forgive as God in Christ forgave me./Amen.
09-12|Grace in My Words|Colossians 4:6|Lord,/let my speech be always with grace, seasoned with salt./When I talk to the people who frustrate me,/let grace come out first./Give me the right answer for every person./Amen.
09-13|The Returning Son|Luke 15:22|Father,/when the son came home You did not lecture him./You put the best robe on him./Thank You for receiving me the same way./Let me walk today wearing Your robe of grace./Amen.
09-14|Undeserved Kindness|Titus 3:5|Lord,/You saved me not because of anything good I did,/but according to Your mercy./Wash me again today/and renew me by Your Holy Spirit./Amen.
09-15|Peter Restored|John 21:17|Jesus,/Peter denied You three times,/and You gave him three chances to say, I love You./Thank You for restoring me after I fail./Lord, You know all things. You know I love You./Amen.
09-16|Forgive Us Our Debts|Matthew 6:12|Father,/forgive me my debts, as I forgive my debtors./I cannot ask for one and refuse the other./Give me the grace to release those who owe me,/as You have released me./Amen.
09-17|His Mercy Endures|Psalm 136:1|Lord,/I give You thanks, for You are good,/and Your mercy endures for ever./Not for a day, not for a season./For ever./Let this truth hold me today./Amen.
09-18|Joseph Forgave|Genesis 50:20|Lord,/Joseph's brothers meant evil against him,/but You meant it for good./Help me see what You can do/with what others meant for harm,/and let me forgive as Joseph did./Amen.
09-19|No Longer Slaves|Galatians 5:1|Lord Jesus,/You set me free./Help me stand fast in that freedom/and not go back under the yoke of guilt and rules./Let me live today as a child of grace./Amen.
09-20|Grace to the Undeserving|Romans 5:8|Father,/You showed Your love for me in that while I was still a sinner,/Christ died for me./You did not wait for me to be good./Let that grace make me gracious to others./Amen.
09-21|A Gentle Restoring|Galatians 6:1|Lord,/when a brother or sister falls,/help me restore them gently,/remembering that I can fall too./Keep me from gossip and pride./Make me a safe place for the broken./Amen.
09-22|Blot Out My Sins|Isaiah 43:25|Lord,/You blot out my sins for Your own sake/and You will not remember them./If You choose to forget,/help me stop rehearsing them./Let me live forgiven./Amen.
09-23|Healing for Shame|Psalm 34:5|Lord,/those who looked to You were radiant,/and their faces were not ashamed./I look to You now./Lift the shame from my face/and let me shine with Your grace./Amen.
09-24|The Thief on the Cross|Luke 23:42-43|Jesus,/the thief said, Lord, remember me,/and You promised him paradise that same day./It is never too late with You./Remember me too,/and remember those I love who are far from You./Amen.
09-25|When I Am Weak|2 Corinthians 12:10|Lord,/Paul learned to say, When I am weak, then am I strong./I do not like my weakness./But if it keeps me leaning on You,/let me make peace with it./Let Your grace be my strength today./Amen.
09-26|Reconciled|2 Corinthians 5:18|Father,/You reconciled me to Yourself through Christ/and gave me the ministry of reconciliation./Show me one broken relationship I can help mend./Give me the courage to take the first step./Amen.
09-27|Forgiving My Parents|Ephesians 6:2-3|Lord,/You tell me to honour my father and mother./Some of what they did hurt me./Help me forgive what needs forgiving/and honour them with grace,/so it may be well with me./Amen.
09-28|Grace Teaches Us|Titus 2:11-12|Father,/Your grace has appeared and it teaches me./It teaches me to say no to ungodliness/and to live soberly and righteously today./Let grace be my teacher, not fear./Amen.
09-29|No Record of Wrongs|1 Corinthians 13:5|Lord,/love keeps no record of wrongs./I have been keeping a list./Today I tear it up./Help me not to rewrite it tomorrow./Amen.
09-30|Amazing Grace|2 Corinthians 9:8|Father,/You are able to make all grace abound toward me./Thank You for every grace I received this month,/the forgiveness, the second chances, the mercy./Let me always have enough to share./Amen.
