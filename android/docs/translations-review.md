# Prayer translations to check before release

These are the prayers in the app's **Pray in your language** screen. Please ask a native speaker, ideally a pastor or church worker, to read each one and mark any wording, spelling or tone-mark corrections.

The English Lord's Prayer is Matthew 6:9-13 (KJV), and the French one is the shared Catholic and Protestant text of 2013. The Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa versions follow the wording used in churches, but each should be checked against the reviewer's own church text. The morning, night and grace prayers were written for PrayerKey.

Corrections go in `ui/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/prayerkey/manna/ui/others/PrayInYourLanguage.kt`.

## The Lord's Prayer

**English** (Matthew 6:9-13, King James Version)

Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen.
