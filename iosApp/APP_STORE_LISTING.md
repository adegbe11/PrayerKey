## Description (4000)

PrayerKey helps you pray every day, read the Bible, and remember what God has done.

PRAY
Each day has a prayer of ten short pulls, about four minutes, so you can pray it through instead of reading about praying. When something is on your heart, write it in a sentence and PrayerKey gives you a prayer built on Scripture. Morning, evening, night and midnight prayers follow the hour you are in.

READ
The whole King James Version is on your phone and works with no signal. Read it as a book that turns like pages, follow a reading plan, or listen to a chapter read aloud. Every day has a written devotional on one passage, with a question to carry and a prayer to finish with.

REMEMBER
Your journal is private. Write a page, mark it as a prayer, and when God answers, mark it answered and write what happened. A week after you pray about something, PrayerKey asks whether it has been answered, so answers are noticed instead of forgotten. Lock the journal with Face ID.

CHURCH
Tap Start during the sermon and PrayerKey writes your notes as the preacher speaks, catching the Bible references it hears. Afterwards you have the main points, the scriptures and a takeaway, ready to read again or share with your small group.

GROW
Named prayer challenges, guided fasting with the verse and focus for each day, Christian meditation with quiet soundscapes, and memory verses that come back when you are about to forget them.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
There is no account and no sign-in. Your journal, prayers, sermon notes and saved verses are kept only on your phone. PrayerKey has no adverts, no tracking and no analytics.

## App Privacy (the questionnaire)

Answer **No, we do not collect data from this app.**

This is accurate: the app has no account, no analytics and no advertising.
Prayer requests and Bible searches are sent to prayerkey.com only to compose
the answer and are not stored; speech is recognised on the phone by Apple's
speech recognition; everything the user keeps stays on the phone. Apple's
definition of "collect" is transmitting data off the device in a way that
lets you or a partner access it beyond servicing the request in real time,
which PrayerKey does not do. If prayerkey.com ever starts storing requests,
this answer must change.

## Notes for App Review

PrayerKey needs no account; every feature is available on first launch.

- Church tab: tap Start to take sermon notes. It asks for the microphone and
  speech recognition, and keeps recording with the screen locked (background
  audio) because a sermon is long and the phone is usually in a pocket.
- Journal: You, Lock journal uses Face ID.
- The Bible, prayers and devotionals are bundled and work offline.
